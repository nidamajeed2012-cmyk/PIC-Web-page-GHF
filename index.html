Who we are

A home for prevention-focused action on AMR.

The Prevention of Infection Committee is a Geneva Health Forum initiative bringing together international expertise from science, clinical practice, public health, policy and civil society.

The PIC Plenary sets priorities and makes decisions. Under the Geneva Health Forum, the Scientific Coordination Team provides neutral scientific and administrative support—developing concept notes, synthesizing evidence and preparing proposals for committee review.

How it started. PIC grew from the November 2025 symposium on infection prevention and AMR, where participants called for a long-term, action-oriented structure able to translate priorities into measurable projects.

Our vision

A world in which infection prevention is recognized, adequately funded and systematically implemented as a primary strategy to control AMR.

Our mission

Bring expertise together to turn scientific and policy insights into actionable recommendations and feasible implementation initiatives.

What we do

We move promising prevention priorities towards action.

PIC is designed as a pragmatic, multi-year programme—not simply an advisory group. Its work connects evidence, committee expertise and implementation opportunities.

Synthesize evidence

Develop concepts

Enable implementation

Connect selected priorities with technical partners, pilot settings, evaluation approaches and routes to scale.
